Privacy Policy

Hamburg 274 (com.hamburg274.piano) · Last updated: November 24, 2026
100% Offline Audio Synthesis · Zero Personal Data Collected · Zero Ads or Trackers

This Privacy Policy explains how Hamburg 274 handles information on Android devices. Hamburg 274 is engineered as a strictly offline concert grand piano instrument and classical practice studio.

1. On-Device Data vs. Cloud Data

  • On-Device Local Data: App preferences (such as dismissal timestamps for Google Play update prompts), imported practice scores, and recorded MIDI/WAV takes remain exclusively on your device.
  • Cloud Data: Hamburg 274 does not require an account, does not operate user tracking servers, and does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data to external servers.

2. Device Permissions & Document Access

Hamburg 274 requests zero dangerous Android permissions (no microphone, camera, location, contacts, phone state, or broad media storage permissions). When you explicitly choose to import a .mid or .musicxml file or export a .mid / 24-bit .wav recording, the app uses Android's built-in Storage Access Framework (SAF) strictly for the specific file you select. Optional USB OTG and Bluetooth LE MIDI keyboards are accessed through Android's standard MidiManager API solely for real-time note and pedal playback.

3. Third-Party Services

Hamburg 274 integrates only the official Google Play In-App Updates library (com.google.android.play:app-update) to check whether a newer app version is available on Google Play. This interaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ads & Analytics

Hamburg 274 contains no advertisements, no ad SDKs, and no third-party behavioral analytics or crash-tracking SDKs.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K / Google Play Families)

Because Hamburg 274 does not collect personal information, does not display advertisements, and includes no social or chat features, it is safe for musicians and students of all ages, including children under 13.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All local settings and session takes reside solely on your device and can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. For complete deletion instructions, visit our Data Deletion Guide.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at phoatman@gmail.com.
