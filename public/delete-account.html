Data & Account Deletion

Hamburg 274 (com.hamburg274.piano) · Last updated: November 24, 2026

Hamburg 274 does not create user accounts, remote profiles, or cloud databases. All app settings and recorded studio takes exist exclusively on your Android device.

1. Delete Data Directly on Your Device

  1. Delete individual studio takes: Open Hamburg 274 → tap the Studio tab → tap the trash icon next to any recorded take.
  2. Delete all local app data: Open Android Settings → Apps → Hamburg 274 → Storage & cache → tap Clear storage (or uninstall the app).
  3. Delete exported files: Any .mid or .wav files you chose to export via Android's Files picker can be removed directly in your device's Files app.

2. What Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted Immediately: Local app preferences (including update prompt preferences) and in-app session recordings are permanently erased upon clearing storage or uninstalling.
  • Retained on Servers: None. Hamburg 274 stores zero user records on external servers.

3. Support Correspondence Deletion (Email Request)

If you previously emailed our support address and would like those support messages deleted, email phoatman@gmail.com with the subject "Hamburg 274 Data Deletion Request". We process all email deletion requests within 7 business days.
