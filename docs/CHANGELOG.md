# Changelog — Hamburg 274

All notable releases for **Hamburg 274** (`com.hamburg274.piano`) are recorded in reverse-chronological order.

---

## v1.1.0 (versionCode 2) — 2026-11-24
- **100% Full-Screen Landscape Concert Grand**: Launches in immersive `sensorLandscape` with transient system bars, a slim 40dp Concert Fallboard Bezel, a single-row 88-key navigator ribbon, and a one-tap **Fullscreen Zen** mode expanding the keybed to 100% of the screen.
- **2+ Full Octaves (`C3–C5`) Default Keybed**: Expanded default landscape span to 15 full-sized white keys with front ivory lip bevels, ebony specular highlights, and an accessible vertical-slide continuous CC64 half-pedal column.
- **Collapsible Quick Mixer**: Tucked Gain, Duplex Resonance, and Hall Reverb into an on-demand inline fader strip on Stage so the keys command maximum vertical travel for `ppp–fff` Y-position touch velocity.
- **Widescreen Practice Alignment**: Proportional Split mode keeps both the engraved Urtext Grand Staff and Falling-Note Waterfall crisp directly above the aligned landscape keybed.
- **Google Play In-App Updates & Compliance**: Added flexible Play Store in-app update prompts with 24-hour dismissal cooldown, manual "Check for Updates", Android 12+ obsidian splash screen, in-app Privacy & Data Safety disclosure, and synchronized version footer.

## v1.0.0 (versionCode 1) — 2026-11-23
- Initial native Android Kotlin + Jetpack Compose release of **Hamburg 274**.
- Native low-latency 48 kHz hybrid acoustic DSP piano synthesizer with 5 instrument voicings, string inharmonicity, sympathetic silent-key bloom, continuous CC64 half-pedaling/re-pedaling, Sostenuto (CC66), Una Corda (CC67), duplex resonance, lid acoustics, and 4 hall reverb spaces.
- 5 historical temperaments (Railsback, Equal, Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti), A4 pitch tuning (415–446 Hz), 3 touch velocity modes, and a draggable 5-point custom velocity curve editor.
- Curated Urtext classical repertoire with Grand Staff & Falling-Note Waterfall, Smart Wait, Hand Isolation, A–B looping, Auto-Ramp (+5%), rubato tolerance, and 4-dimensional expressive grading.
- Studio MIDI Recorder with non-destructive Re-Voice audition and Android SAF `.mid` / 24-bit stereo `.wav` export plus `.mid` / `.musicxml` score import.
- Native Android USB OTG & Bluetooth LE `MidiManager` support, OEM 3-finger chord diagnostic, Pro Audio diagnostics, and battery-saving OLED Sound-Module mode.
