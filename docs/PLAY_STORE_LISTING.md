## Full Description (2,168 / 4,000 characters)

```text
Sit at a full-screen 274 cm concert grand piano engineered specifically for classical musicians, conservatory students, and expressive mobile play. Hamburg 274 pairs a low-latency offline hybrid acoustic DSP engine with an immersive full-screen landscape keybed, Urtext Grand Staff practice, and native USB OTG & Bluetooth LE MIDI support.

CONCERT ACOUSTIC DSP ENGINE
• Five sculpted keyboard voicings: Hamburg 274 Concert Grand, New York Studio 274, Intimate Felt Upright, Baroque Harpsichord, and 1820s Viennese Fortepiano.
• Physical string inharmonicity, continuous velocity-to-timbre shading, silent key press sympathetic bloom, duplex comb resonance, and adjustable hammer/damper mechanical action noise.
• Authentic three-pedal physics: continuous CC64 half-pedaling and re-pedaling, Sostenuto (CC66), and Una Corda (CC67).
• Concert Lid positions (Full, Half, Closed), Pianist vs. Audience stereo perspective, and four acoustic spaces (Philharmonic Hall, Chamber Recital Room, Intimate Salon, Dry Close Studio).
• Five historical & concert temperaments (Railsback Concert Stretch, Equal, Werckmeister III, Kirnberger III, Vallotti) with adjustable A4 reference pitch from 415 Hz to 446 Hz.

100% FULL-SCREEN LANDSCAPE KEYBED
• Full-width 88-key touch instrument with Single, Dual-Manual, and face-to-face Duet layouts, Key-Lock vs. Glissando toggle, interactive octave compass navigator, and a one-tap Fullscreen Zen mode.
• Three touch velocity engines (Vertical Y-Position ppp–fff, Pressure/Contact Fusion, Fixed) plus a draggable 5-point custom velocity curve editor.
• Ergonomic thumb-slide vertical CC64 half-pedal column built directly beside the keys.

URTEXT PRACTICE & 4D EXPRESSIVE GRADING
• Curated public-domain classical works (Bach, Beethoven, Chopin, Mozart, Debussy, Satie) plus on-device Standard MIDI (.mid) and MusicXML (.musicxml) score import.
• Engraved parchment Grand Staff synchronized with a keybed-aligned Falling-Note Waterfall (Urtext, Split, and Waterfall views).
• Smart Wait mode, Hand Isolation (Both, RH, LH), A–B measure looping, Auto-Ramp (+5% tempo per loop), adjustable rubato tolerance, and 4-dimensional grading across Pitch, Rubato Rhythm, Dynamics, and Syncopated CC64 Pedaling.

STUDIO RECORDER & NATIVE ANDROID MIDI
• Records true finger Note-On/Off durations separately from continuous CC64, CC66, and CC67 pedal lanes.
• Non-destructive Re-Voice audition across all five instruments and lossless Standard MIDI (.mid) or 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV export.
• Plug-and-play Android USB OTG & Bluetooth LE MIDI, interactive 3-finger OEM gesture diagnostic, real-time audio latency telemetry, and pure-black OLED Sound-Module mode.

OFFLINE-FIRST & PRIVACY-RESPECTING
• 100% offline DSP synthesis — no mandatory login, no mid-play ads, no analytics trackers, and zero microphone, camera, or storage permissions.
```
